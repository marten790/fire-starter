# Requirements discovery questions

Answer these in chat (or directly in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` under **Answers**). The more specific you are, the better the dashboard fits your real kiln routine.

---

## 1. About you & your kiln

1. **Kiln make and model** (if known) — e.g. Skutt, L&L, home-built?
2. **Kiln size** — how many shelves? Top-loading or front-loading?
3. **Fuel** — electric, gas, or wood?
4. **Approximate age / condition** — new, old elements, any known cold spots?
5. **Temperature meter** — type (infrared gun, probe, analog dial)? Units **°C or °F**?
6. **Cone brand** — Orton or other? Which cone numbers do you normally use for **bisque** and **glaze**?
7. **Where is the kiln** — garage, shed, studio? **Wi‑Fi / cell signal** there?
8. **iPad model** — helps us test layout (e.g. iPad Air, Pro 11")?

---

## 2. Your bisque firing workflow (step by step)

9. **Typical bisque target** — cone number and approximate peak on your meter?
10. **How long** does a typical bisque take (hours)?
11. **Dial routine** — describe how you use the knob through the fire (e.g. low for first hour, then…).
12. **How often** do you check the meter during bisque — every 15 min, 30 min, when it "looks" right?
13. **Venting** — peephole plugs, lid propped, fan? When do you change venting?
14. **When do you shut off** — cone fall, meter reading, time, or combination?
15. **Cooling** — when do you open the kiln? Any rules you follow?
16. **What goes wrong** sometimes on bisque — cracks, explosion, underfire?

---

## 3. Your glaze (main) firing workflow

17. **Typical glaze target** — cone number (e.g. cone 6) and peak on meter?
18. **How long** does a typical glaze fire take?
19. **Dial routine** — different from bisque? Soak at top?
20. **Cone placement** — one pack per shelf, one for whole kiln, eye level?
21. **Glazes / clay bodies** — do you use many recipes or a few staples? Want to log names per firing?
22. **Shut-off rule** — target cone bent how far? Guard cone still standing?
23. **Cooling** — fast, slow, or natural? Ever hold at a temperature?
24. **Common glaze problems** — pinholes, running, crazing, underfire?

---

## 4. What must be on the dashboard (MVP)

Think about **standing next to a hot kiln** with gloves on.

### During an active firing

25. **Start firing** — one tap to start bisque vs glaze?
26. **Elapsed timer** — do you want a visible clock since start?
27. **Log temperature** — how should entry work? Big number pad? Last reading shown?
28. **Log dial position** — numeric (0–10), low/med/high, or free text?
29. **Quick notes** — voice-to-text, short text, or preset chips ("ramped up", "checking cones")?
30. **Reminders** — should the app nudge you at intervals ("check meter") or at temperatures you define?
31. **Checklist** — e.g. "vent open", "cones in", "kiln sitter set" before start?

### After the firing (unload / review)

32. **Cone results** — per shelf or one overall? Under / perfect / over?
33. **Photos** — cones, glazed pieces, shelf load?
34. **Peak temperature** — single field or highest logged during fire?
35. **Outcome rating** — thumbs up/down or 1–5 stars?
36. **Load record** — count of pieces, sketch shelf layout, or text only?

### History & reference

37. **History list** — filter by bisque vs glaze? search by date?
38. **Compare firings** — need side-by-side two past fires?
39. **"Copy last firing"** — start a new fire pre-filled from previous schedule notes?
40. **Maintenance** — track firings since element replacement? reminder at N firings?

---

## 5. Nice-to-have (later, not MVP)

41. **Clay & glaze library** — save bodies and glazes with links to firings?
42. **Export** — PDF log for your records?
43. **Multiple kilns** — only one kiln forever, or might you add another?
44. **Sharing** — anyone else needs access (partner, studio)?
45. **Weather / season** — care about logging ambient conditions?
46. **Alerts** — sound or notification when reminder fires (iPad locked)?

---

## 6. Design & Figma

47. **Figma link** — can you share view access to your design system file?
48. **Light or dark UI** — preference for kiln room?
49. **Brand name** — stay with **Fire Starter** or another name?
50. **Reference apps** — any dashboard or app whose *feel* you like (not necessarily pottery)?

## 7. Success criteria

51. **After 3 months**, what would make you say "this was worth building"?
52. **Biggest pain today** — paper notebook, Notes app, memory, something else?
53. **Anything you tried before** that didn't work?
